Download on the App Store

Every Letter Distilled Into 5 Verified Facts

Point your iPhone camera at any physical letter or import a PDF. Letter Brief extracts the five facts that matter and highlights the exact source sentence on the page when you tap any row:

  • What it is — Sender and document category (Citation, Medical Bill / EOB, IRS Notice, Lease, Collection Letter).
  • What they ask — The core action required in plain English.
  • Date — Extracted due date or response deadline with 1-tap .ics Calendar alert export.
  • Amount — Exact dollar figure due, balance, or penalty.
  • If you don't — Late fees, interest, or legal consequences stated on the page.

Action Plan, Reply Letter Templates, Sign & PDF Export

  • Action Plan & Draft Reply Letter: Generates a 3-step checklist and ready-to-copy written response/dispute letter tailored to your notice type.
  • Interactive 4-Category Sample Library: Test a Parking Citation ($65.00), Medical Bill ($240.00), IRS Notice CP14 ($1,180.00), and Lease Renewal ($1,850.00) right from the Home screen.
  • Sign & Export Searchable PDF: Place your signature or export a searchable PDF with an optional 5-Fact Summary Cover Page.

100% On-Device Privacy

Letter Brief reads your pages locally on your iPhone using Apple Vision. No account is required, and your documents never leave your device unless you choose to export or share a PDF.
