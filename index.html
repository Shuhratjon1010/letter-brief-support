What Letter Brief does

Scan or import a letter and get five plain answers from the words on the page: what it is, what they ask, the date, the amount, and what happens if you don’t. Tap an answer to see where it came from on your page. Sign with your finger and save a separate signed PDF. Text is read on your iPhone; the page image is not uploaded.

Common questions

A row says “Not found in this letter.”

The app only shows facts printed on the page and never guesses. Try a sharper scan in good light, with the whole page in the frame, or import the original PDF.

The camera won’t open.

Open the Settings app, tap Letter Brief, and turn on Camera. You can also import a photo or a PDF instead.

How do I delete a letter?

Swipe it away under Recent. Deleting the app deletes every saved letter.

Is my first brief free?

Yes. Your first successful brief is free. After that, a subscription unlocks unlimited briefs. The price and the free-trial length are shown in the app before you subscribe.

How do I restore a subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Letter Brief, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings inside the app.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Letter Brief. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this advice?

No. The brief summarizes the words on the page. It is a reading aid, not advice. Check the original letter before you act.
